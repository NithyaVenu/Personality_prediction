About Us

Our project Personality Prediction System is done using Visual Studio Code, Django, Python, PostGreSQL, and Google Forms.

The main aim of this project is to provide fast services to the Human Resources Department and the candidates. With the help of this portal the HR Department can shortlist the candidates easily according to their(candidate's) personality. The candidate's personality will be predicted based on the Big Five traits of Personality- OCEAN(Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism).

The result will be presented in form of graphs to both the Admin and the candidate via Google Forms and Spreadsheets. Admin can also add quizzes and view the responses and respective results efficiently with the help of Google Forms.
